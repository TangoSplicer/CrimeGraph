# CrimeGraph Competitive Capability Benchmark

**Purpose:** This benchmark records documented patterns from comparable investigation, digital-evidence, and intelligence-analysis products. It is a product-research input, not an endorsement or a specification to copy. CrimeGraph will retain its offline-first, analyst-controlled, device-bound security model.

> **Scope boundary:** Vendor claims are treated as descriptions of their products. CrimeGraph will implement original workflows only where they strengthen provenance, traceability, field safety, or analyst-controlled casework without enabling opaque person-level scoring, covert collection, or unattended case transfer.

## Sources examined

| Product or source | Documented capability pattern | CrimeGraph interpretation |
|---|---|---|
| [NiCE Investigate][1] | Case folders unify heterogeneous digital evidence; automated case building, evidence connections, redaction, disclosure, and structured workflows are emphasized. | Maintain a local case dossier, but make all export, correlation, and review user-mediated. Prioritize a court-ready case packet, evidence relationships, and deliberate redaction review—not automated evidence recommendations. |
| [Axon digital evidence guide][2] | Original-file preservation, source metadata, direct field capture, searchable evidence, transcript review, tags, linked cases, derivatives, and redaction are documented as evidence-management concerns. | Preserve originals and provenance; add local derivative manifests and transcript/annotation workflows only when an operator explicitly creates them. Do not add cloud upload or automatic collection. |
| [Kaseware][3] | Unified case, document, evidence, task, operational, link-analysis, and geospatial workflows are presented as one investigation workspace. | Extend CrimeGraph’s graph and field tasks with explicit investigation plans, geospatial observations, and briefing views while retaining local access controls. |
| [Case Closed][4] | Full case lifecycle, tips/leads, physical-evidence barcodes, court tracking, reports, link analysis, role-based sharing, and audit controls are presented as integrated modules. | Add a local leads register, exhibit labels, court/disclosure preparation, and provenance-first reporting. Do not replicate confidential-informant reliability scoring, payment tooling, or unmanaged cross-agency sharing. |
| [DataWalk][5] | Visual queries, geospatial/flow analysis, saved reusable queries, data lineage, and granular security are positioned as intelligence-analysis tooling. | Build explainable saved local queries, entity-resolution review queues, temporal/geospatial filters, and graph paths. No autonomous data ingestion, automated entity merge, or generative conclusions. |
| [SoundThinking CaseBuilder analysis][6] | Configurable action checklists, investigative stages, caseload visibility, structured folders, reporting, and role controls are identified as investigation-management patterns. | Implement optional case playbooks and milestone checklists that record completion evidence, blockers, and accountable sign-off—not productivity ranking or performance scoring. |

## Capability themes and original opportunity

| Theme | Market pattern | CrimeGraph’s original, safer direction |
|---|---|---|
| Investigation planning | Workflow stages, task lists, deadlines, and case status. | A local **Case Playbook**: configurable, evidence-linked milestones, blockers, and review gates. It describes work completion; it does not rate personnel or predict outcomes. |
| Evidence review | Tags, transcripts, playback, redaction, original-file retention, and export. | A **Derivative & Annotation Ledger**: explicit, hash-linked records of thumbnails, extracts, transcripts, annotations, and redactions tied to an original evidence item. |
| Court and disclosure | Report generation, court tracking, case folders, secure sharing. | A **Briefing & Disclosure Builder**: reproducible, marked, redacted, user-mediated case packets with a source manifest and a court-event checklist. |
| Leads and intake | Tip portals, intake queues, complaints, and task routing. | A device-local **Lead Register** with source/provenance, sensitivity marking, triage state, and explicit convert-to-intelligence action. No public upload endpoint or background transmission. |
| Spatial and temporal review | Maps, timelines, time-synced media, and flow analysis. | An **Observation Map** with accuracy/uncertainty, source and observed-time filters, and explicit analytical notes. No live tracking, geofencing, or predictive hotspotting. |
| Graph intelligence | Visual query, entity resolution, path exploration, analytics. | An **Explainable Query Workbench**: saved local filters, shortest paths, source-constrained views, and human-approved duplicate resolution. |
| Evidence logistics | Barcode labels, package location, property status, custody history. | A **Local Exhibit Register** with QR labels, container/storage records, handling events, and manifest exports. Scanning may be offline; it must not open remote transfers. |
| Assurance and administration | Granular roles, audit, workload visibility, enterprise reporting. | A **Case Health & Readiness** view that surfaces provenance gaps, pending reviews, blocked milestones, disclosure state, audit verification, and storage assurance—without evaluating investigator performance. |

## Explicit exclusions

CrimeGraph will not adopt automated person-level risk or reliability scoring, facial recognition, biometric matching, live surveillance, predictive policing, public evidence-upload portals, cloud synchronization, unattended inter-device transfer, or unreviewed Bluetooth/mesh case exchange. These are incompatible with the current approved security and governance boundary.
